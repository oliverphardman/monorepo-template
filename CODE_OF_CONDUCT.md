# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers privately, through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.
